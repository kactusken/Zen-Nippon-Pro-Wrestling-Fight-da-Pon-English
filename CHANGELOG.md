# Changelog

## v1.0
- First release: the whole game in English - story dialogue, card labels, wrestler names, menus and prompts.
- New variable-width font, and the ROM expanded to 16 Mbit to hold the English text.
- Fixes two bugs of the original game: a portrait drawn over a wrestler's sprite, and a gauge left on screen before a scene's first line.
- Optional second patch, (Debug Menu): restores the developers' debug menu. Checked in automated emulator runs; not yet played by hand.
